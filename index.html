Wall Street English Learning & Development November 2026

The onboarding redesign gives our centers back 17,200 hours a year

Onboarding across the five role paths dropped from 190 to about 91 hours, a 52% cut, with the same learning objectives. The average new hire now reaches the floor about 3.0 working days sooner.

These are approximate, conservative figures based on estimates. The instructor-led part of onboarding is being redesigned and optimised, and we will review these figures in depth. The leadership deck rounds this down to about €200k (about 16,500 hours).

How the time was saved

The time savings come from three changes. Every path keeps the same learning objectives.

Changing platform

Training moved from LearnUpon to 360Learning. Each role now has one self-serve Learning Library path that new hires work through at their own pace, in order.

Redesigning manager touchpoints

Before, almost every module had its own instructor-led follow-up. Now all in-person time with the manager is grouped into about 8 hours per path (about 2.5 hours for Service Managers and Center Directors), ending in a role-play and a performance check. This part is being redesigned.

Refactoring existing training

Overlapping modules were merged and trimmed. Seven essentials courses became two 20-minute modules, and the sales line was consolidated into three. Each original KPI is mapped to a module in the new catalogue.

Trainer and manager time

Follow-up sessions after each module are now one block of instructor-led time per path

€79k

In March 2026, nearly every module came with an instructor-led follow-up in center. E-modules are now self-serve, and in-person time is grouped into about 8 hours of instructor-led sessions and observation per path (about 2.5 hours for Service Managers and Center Directors). That segment is being redesigned, so this figure will change. Every follow-up removed also saves a trainer, Service Manager or Center Director hour, often the most expensive hours in the center.

Faster time to productivity

Sales Consultants start booking and closing contracts days sooner

€115k

A Sales Consultant finishes onboarding about 3.3 working days sooner, so they start booking and closing contracts sooner. This uses the Sales Consultant share from the hire mix (219 hires a year). Teachers and Coaches also start delivering classes and sessions sooner, which isn't counted here.

Consistency and lower delivery load

One self-serve path per role, delivered the same way in every territory

Not costed

Each role has one path, delivered the same way in 32 countries, so quality varies less from center to center. There's less local scheduling, new hires don't wait for a trainer to be free, and there are fewer sessions to coordinate.

Most of this value sits in franchisee and territory P&Ls, not in Wall Street English International's own costs. Read it as time and value returned to the network, not as a central budget saving.
